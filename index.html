About VibeType AI
Speak into Existence
VibeType AI is designed to save you time. Stop typing like a grandpa and start speaking naturally — it handles transcription and types directly into any app. It is also a system-wide writing helper: stay in flow, polish text faster, and get cleaner output in docs, email, Slack, IDEs, and browsers.
Press the global hotkey to start recording; it automatically types the result into your active application. Press your recording shortcut to capture speech, transcribe it, and insert text into the focused window — no copy-paste step. Great for long messages, notes, and first drafts when typing would slow you down.
Press Ctrl + Shift + E to open the Assistant (Mac: ⌘⇧E) with text selected in another app. Use it to fix grammar, change tone, or summarize highlighted text. It can run a quick quality check, then actions like fix & polish, professional tone, summarize, draft reply, shorten, or expand, plus a custom instruction — paste results back when you are done. Requires an OpenAI API key in Settings.
Use local Whisper for privacy or OpenAI for maximum accuracy and cloud features. Pick the default in Settings; tune language, model, and typing behavior (e.g. auto-type after transcription). The Assistant and recording use global shortcuts. The app can live in the menu bar / system tray — tweak what is enabled in Settings if something conflicts with another tool.

Privacy First
Local transcription is available via Whisper — your audio can stay on your machine. OpenAI modes may send audio or text to OpenAI per their policies; use Settings to control keys and features.
